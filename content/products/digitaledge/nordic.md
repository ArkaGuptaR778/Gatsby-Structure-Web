## intro
title: RS DigitalEdge™ one orchestration layer for *a connected Nordic payment landscape*

The Nordic payments landscape is increasingly shaped by instant payments, account-to-account flows, common standards and cross-border interoperability.

RS DigitalEdge™ helps institutions bring these payment environments together through a single orchestration layer. Its API-first, ISO 20022-ready architecture supports payment routing, message transformation and interoperability across evolving instant-payment ecosystems while allowing existing infrastructure to remain in place.

## metrics
- **40% Faster** — Scheme Integration — Accelerate adoption of evolving payment schemes and payment capabilities.
- **30% Lower** — Processing Cost — Optimize payment routing and channel selection.
- **99.9%** — Platform Availability — Support resilient payment processing across connected payment environments.

## why
eyebrow: Why RS DigitalEdge™
title: *Orchestrating the move* toward connected, instant payments

As payment ecosystems converge around SEPA, instant payments and emerging real-time infrastructures, interoperability becomes as important as transaction speed.

DigitalEdge provides a common orchestration layer for these environments, helping institutions connect payment flows, apply routing logic and manage ISO 20022 messages without rebuilding their underlying systems.

Its composable architecture also supports a more gradual modernization path—allowing new payment capabilities to be introduced as market requirements evolve.

## capabilities
- **Instant Payment Orchestration** — Manage payment flows across instant and account-based payment environments through one orchestration layer.
- **Multi-Rail Connectivity** — Connect real-time, card, account-based and alternative payment flows without creating isolated integrations.
- **Intelligent Routing** — Use configurable rules and real-time intelligence to select appropriate payment paths.
- **ISO 20022 & Interoperability** — Support ISO 20022-based payment environments while maintaining connectivity with existing message formats.
- **Cross-Border Orchestration** — Support interoperability across domestic and international payment corridors.
- **Low-Code Payment Workflows** — Configure and adapt payment processes with reduced engineering dependency.
- **Resilience & Failover** — Automated retries, exception management and failover help maintain continuity across payment flows.
- **Telemetry & Operational Analytics** — Monitor payment journeys, transaction states and orchestration performance through live operational data.
