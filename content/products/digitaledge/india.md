## intro
title: RS DigitalEdge™ orchestrating *India's next phase of digital payments*

India's payment ecosystem has demonstrated what happens when digital payments are built for scale. The next challenge is connecting that scale across rails, institutions and emerging payment use cases.

RS DigitalEdge™ provides the orchestration layer to connect UPI, IMPS, NEFT, RTGS and other payment flows through a unified architecture. With API-first integration, intelligent routing and ISO 20022 readiness, it helps institutions modernize without replacing the systems already powering their payment operations.

## metrics
- **40% Faster** — Scheme Integration — Accelerate adoption of new payment capabilities and payment schemes.
- **30% Lower** — Processing Cost — Optimize routing and channel selection across payment flows.
- **99.9%** — Platform Availability — Designed for resilient, continuous payment operations.

## why
eyebrow: Why RS DigitalEdge™
title: *Built for a payment ecosystem* that moves at India's speed

India's payment landscape spans high-volume digital payments, traditional banking rails and increasingly connected cross-border corridors. DigitalEdge brings these flows together through one orchestration layer, helping institutions connect existing infrastructure with new payment capabilities.

From UPI, IMPS, NEFT and RTGS to cross-border corridors such as UPI–PayNow, the platform provides the connectivity, transformation and routing capabilities needed to move between payment environments without creating another layer of fragmentation.

## capabilities
- **Multi-Rail Payment Orchestration** — Connect and orchestrate payment flows across UPI, IMPS, NEFT, RTGS and other payment environments.
- **Intelligent Routing** — Apply configurable business rules and real-time intelligence to select the appropriate payment path.
- **API-First Connectivity** — Expose payment capabilities through APIs and integrate with existing banking and enterprise systems.
- **ISO 20022 Enablement** — Support ISO 20022 adoption while maintaining interoperability with existing message formats.
- **Cross-Border Connectivity** — Support payment corridor connectivity such as UPI–PayNow and other emerging cross-border flows.
- **Low-Code Configuration** — Create and adapt payment workflows faster without extensive engineering changes.
- **Exception & Failover Management** — Automate retries, exception handling and failover to maintain continuity across payment journeys.
- **Operational Visibility** — Track payment events, transaction states and processing performance through reporting and telemetry.
