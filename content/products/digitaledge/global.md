## intro
title: RS DigitalEdge™ is *the unified payment modernization platform*

Payments are evolving at remarkable speed, connecting customers, banks, merchants, and payment rails like never before. Banks and financial institutions need the flexibility to support real-time, card, account-based, and emerging payment flows all through infrastructure that can keep pace.

RS DigitalEdge™ brings these moving parts together through a single intelligent orchestration layer, combining smart routing, real-time decisioning, message transformation, compliance, and resilience. Its API-first, cloud-ready architecture helps institutions modernize payment capabilities while extending existing systems rather than replacing them.

## metrics
- **40% Faster** — Scheme Integration — Accelerate the adoption of new payment schemes and overlay services.
- **30% Lower** — Processing Cost — Optimize payment flows through intelligent channel and network selection.
- **99.9%** — Platform Availability — Support resilient, always-on payment operations.

## why
eyebrow: Why RS DigitalEdge™
title: *RS DigitalEdge™ orchestrates payments* across rails, systems and business flows

Modern payment infrastructure cannot afford to operate as a collection of disconnected rails. DigitalEdge creates a unified orchestration layer that connects payment networks, transforms messages, applies business rules, and intelligently routes transactions while working alongside existing payment and core systems.

Its composable architecture allows institutions to modernize progressively, adding capabilities as their payment needs evolve.

## capabilities
- **Multi-Rail Orchestration** — Orchestrate payment flows across real-time, card, account-based, and alternate payment networks through a unified platform.
- **Intelligent Routing** — Use AI insights and configurable business rules to determine the right payment path, improving transaction success and optimizing processing costs.
- **API-First Integration** — Connect payment rails, core systems, ERP platforms, and fraud/risk systems through a streamlined API layer.
- **ISO 20022 & Interoperability** — Native ISO 20022 readiness with built-in adapters for ISO 8583 and native message formats.
- **Low-Code Orchestration** — Build and modify payment workflows with low-code configurability, reducing engineering dependency and accelerating change.
- **Composable Architecture** — Core, Mantle and Surface layers allow institutions to introduce capabilities incrementally without disrupting existing systems.
- **Real-Time Resilience** — Automated retries, failover and exception handling help keep payment flows running through operational disruptions.
- **Analytics & Transaction Visibility** — Live telemetry, reporting and transaction lineage provide visibility into how payment flows perform, fail and recover.
