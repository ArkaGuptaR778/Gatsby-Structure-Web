## intro
title: RS DigitalEdge™ orchestrating *America's move to real-time payments*

The U.S. payment landscape is moving across ACH, cards, FedNow, RTP and emerging real-time payment use cases, creating a growing need to connect different payment environments without adding more operational complexity.

RS DigitalEdge™ provides a unified orchestration layer across these rails, combining intelligent routing, ISO 20022 message transformation, API-first connectivity and real-time exception handling. Institutions can modernize payment capabilities while continuing to work with their existing core and payment infrastructure.

## metrics
- **40% Faster** — Scheme Integration — Accelerate adoption of new payment schemes and overlay capabilities.
- **30% Lower** — Processing Cost — Optimize payment routing and channel selection.
- **99.9%** — Platform Availability — Support resilient payment operations across always-on payment environments.

## why
eyebrow: Why RS DigitalEdge™
title: *From ACH and cards to FedNow and RTP*, connect the payment landscape

U.S. institutions increasingly need to manage traditional and real-time payment environments side by side. DigitalEdge creates a common orchestration layer that connects these flows rather than forcing each rail to operate as another isolated integration.

With FedNow, RTP, ACH, cards and SWIFT MX within the modernization landscape, DigitalEdge supports message transformation, intelligent routing, exception handling and ISO 20022 interoperability through a unified architecture.

## capabilities
- **FedNow & RTP Orchestration** — Connect and orchestrate real-time payment flows alongside existing payment environments.
- **Multi-Rail Connectivity** — Bring ACH, RTP, FedNow, cards and other payment flows into a unified orchestration layer.
- **Intelligent Routing** — Use configurable rules and real-time intelligence to select efficient payment paths.
- **ISO 20022 Enablement** — Support ISO 20022-based payment modernization while maintaining interoperability with existing formats.
- **API-First Integration** — Connect payment switches, core systems, ERP platforms and fraud/risk systems through APIs.
- **Overlay & Payment Innovation** — Support capabilities such as Request-to-Pay, Confirmation of Payee and other payment overlays.
- **Real-Time Resilience** — Automated retries, failover and exception handling help maintain continuity across payment flows.
- **Liquidity & Operational Visibility** — Provide visibility into payment flows, liquidity positions and orchestration performance.
