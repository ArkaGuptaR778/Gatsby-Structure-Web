## intro
title: RS DigitalEdge™ connecting *Canada's evolving payment landscape*

Canada's payment modernization journey brings together new real-time capabilities, established payment infrastructure and growing demand for cross-border interoperability.

RS DigitalEdge™ creates a unified orchestration layer across RTR, ACH and connected payment environments, helping financial institutions modernize without disrupting existing infrastructure. With ISO 20022 readiness, intelligent routing and real-time orchestration, institutions can connect domestic and cross-border payment flows through a common architecture.

## metrics
- **40% Faster** — Scheme Integration — Accelerate readiness for evolving payment schemes and payment services.
- **30% Lower** — Processing Cost — Optimize routing and payment-channel selection.
- **99.9%** — Platform Availability — Support resilient payment operations across connected rails.

## why
eyebrow: Why RS DigitalEdge™
title: *From RTR readiness to cross-border connectivity*, one orchestration layer

Modernization becomes more complex when institutions have to operate new real-time capabilities alongside existing payment infrastructure.

RS DigitalEdge™ brings these environments together. It can orchestrate RTR, ACH, and FedNow-connected flows, while providing visibility across payment processing, routing, liquidity, and exceptions.

For institutions expanding beyond domestic payments, its ISO 20022-ready architecture also creates a foundation for interoperability across CAD/USD corridors and other cross-border payment flows.

## capabilities
- **RTR & Multi-Rail Orchestration** — Connect and orchestrate RTR alongside existing payment environments through a unified layer.
- **Intelligent Routing** — Apply configurable routing rules to optimize payment paths based on business requirements.
- **Cross-Border Connectivity** — Support connected corridors such as RTR–FedNow and other cross-border payment flows.
- **ISO 20022 Interoperability** — Transform, validate and enrich messages while maintaining interoperability with existing formats.
- **API-First Integration** — Connect payment infrastructure, core banking, ERP and other enterprise systems through APIs.
- **Liquidity & Payment Visibility** — Provide visibility into payment flows, funding positions and operational performance.
- **Exception & Failover Management** — Manage retries, exceptions and failover across payment journeys.
- **Composable Modernization** — Add new capabilities progressively without a disruptive rip-and-replace transformation.
