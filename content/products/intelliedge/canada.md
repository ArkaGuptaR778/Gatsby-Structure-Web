## tab-bank
label: For Bank
title: *Built for* banks where fraud can move across payment rails.

Canadian banks operate across Interac e-Transfer, cards, wires and emerging real-time payment environments. Fraud protection therefore needs to connect signals rather than treat each payment channel separately.

RS IntelliEdge™ brings behavioural, transaction and ecosystem intelligence together to help banks identify APP fraud, account takeover and mule activity while maintaining visibility across different customer and payment journeys.

## tab-bank-why
title: Cross-rail fraud intelligence for Canada’s evolving payment environment.

RS IntelliEdge™ helps banks connect transaction, behavioural and ecosystem intelligence across Interac e-Transfer, real-time payments, cards and other channels. By bringing these signals into a unified intelligence layer, it helps identify patterns that isolated systems may miss and supports explainable risk decisions as payment activity moves across rails.

## tab-central
label: For Central
title: *Built for* Canada’s changing payment landscape.

Canada’s payment ecosystem is evolving across Real-Time Rail, Interac e-Transfer, cards and wires, creating a need for fraud intelligence that can extend across established and emerging payment channels.

RS IntelliEdge™ brings these signals together through real-time scoring, behavioural intelligence and federated learning, helping central infrastructures identify fraud patterns that may not be visible within a single rail or institution.

## tab-central-why
title: Connecting fraud intelligence across Canada’s payment rails.

RS IntelliEdge™ brings real-time fraud, risk and AML intelligence across RTR, Interac e-Transfer and other payment channels, combining AI/ML, behavioural analytics, rules and federated learning. It helps payment ecosystems connect fraud signals across participating institutions, strengthen cross-rail visibility, and support fast, explainable decisions as Canada’s payment infrastructure evolves.
