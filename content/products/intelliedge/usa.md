## intro
title: RS IntelliEdge™ is RS Software's *AI/ML-driven* fraud and risk platform

RS IntelliEdge™ is RS Software's AI/ML-driven fraud and risk management solution designed for real-time protection of digital payments. It adapts to evolving threats, ensuring secure, intelligent monitoring across all transaction types. Whether you're managing national payment systems or banking operations, RS IntelliEdge™ has you covered.

## tab-bank
label: For Bank
title: *Built for* U.S. banks where identity cannot be a one-time check.

Instant and A2A payments can settle in seconds, leaving limited time to react after a transaction is initiated. RS IntelliEdge™ brings continuous risk assessment into the payment flow, using behavioural, device and contextual signals to help identify APP scams, account takeover and identity-led threats.

Through the ChainIT alliance, the intelligence layer can work alongside existing fraud systems while the financial institution retains control of the final action.

## tab-bank-why
title: Continuous identity and fraud intelligence for U.S. A2A payments.

RS IntelliEdge™ continuously evaluates transaction and identity risk using behavioural, device and contextual signals. Through the ChainIT alliance, this intelligence can work alongside existing fraud tools for A2A payments, helping surface the risk, the reason behind the decision and an auditable record while allowing the financial institution to retain control over the final action.

## tab-bank-capabilities
- **Real-Time Fraud Detection** — Assess transaction risk in milliseconds and identify suspicious activity before funds leave the account.
- **A2A & Instant Payment Protection** — Extend fraud intelligence to account-to-account payments where transactions can settle instantly and irrevocably.
- **Behavioural Analytics & Customer 360** — Combine transaction, behavioural, device and contextual signals to identify deviations from normal activity.

## tab-central
label: For Central
title: *Built for* America’s expanding real-time payment ecosystem.

The U.S. payment landscape is moving toward faster A2A and instant payments through FedNow® Service and the RTP® network, where transactions can settle quickly and may be difficult to reverse.

RS IntelliEdge™ creates an intelligence layer across payment channels, helping payment ecosystems connect fraud signals across institutions and identify risk that may remain hidden within individual rails.

## tab-central-why
title: Intelligence for a payment landscape moving from instant payments to continuous risk assessment.

RS IntelliEdge™ brings real-time fraud, risk and AML intelligence across the U.S. payment ecosystem, combining AI/ML, behavioural analytics, rules and federated learning. Through the ChainIT alliance, this approach extends to risk-based authentication for A2A payments, helping institutions continuously assess identity and fraud risk alongside existing fraud systems.
