## tab-bank
label: For Bank
title: *Built for* banks operating at India’s UPI payment speed.

With UPI, cards, A2A and other digital payment channels driving increasingly connected payment journeys, banks need fraud protection that can see beyond individual transactions.

RS IntelliEdge™ combines behavioural intelligence, AI/ML and real-time scoring to identify suspicious activity across customer and payment journeys, helping banks respond quickly while protecting legitimate transactions.

## tab-bank-why
title: Real-time fraud intelligence for India’s UPI-led digital payment landscape.

RS IntelliEdge™ helps banks assess transaction risk as payments happen, combining behavioural analytics, AI/ML, rules and ecosystem intelligence. It helps identify fraud patterns across UPI, cards, A2A and other payment channels, while supporting explainable decisions, targeted intervention and a more continuous view of customer and transaction risk.

## tab-bank-capabilities
- **Real-Time Fraud Detection** — Score transactions at payment speed and identify suspicious activity before funds leave the account.
- **Cross-Channel Protection** — Monitor fraud across digital and banking channels rather than treating each channel as a separate risk environment.
- **Behavioural Analytics & Customer 360** — Build a richer picture of customer behaviour using transaction, device and behavioural signals.
- **APP, ATO & Mule Detection** — Detect authorised push payment fraud, account takeover and mule activity using behavioural and AI-powered analysis.
- **Explainable AI (XAI)** — Give fraud teams clear explanations for risk decisions and maintain audit-ready trails.
- **Adaptive AI/ML** — Continuously adapt detection as fraud patterns evolve.
- **Case Management & AML Assist** — Bring fraud investigation and AML workflows together with case management and entity-link analysis.
- **API-First Integration** — Extend fraud intelligence across existing banking and payment infrastructure.

## tab-central
label: For Central
title: *Built for* India’s UPI-scale payment ecosystem.

India’s payment ecosystem operates at extraordinary volume, with UPI at the centre of everyday digital payments. RS IntelliEdge™ brings real-time fraud intelligence across the ecosystem, helping central infrastructure identify threats across participating institutions and payment channels.
