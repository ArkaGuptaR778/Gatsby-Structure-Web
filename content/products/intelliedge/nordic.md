## tab-bank
label: For Bank
title: *Built for* banks in an A2A-first world.

Digital payments have made transactions faster and more convenient, but the same speed can amplify social engineering, APP fraud and account takeover risks.

RS IntelliEdge™ combines behavioural, transaction, device and identity intelligence to help Nordic banks identify suspicious activity across A2A, wallets, cards and digital banking channels, while maintaining a smoother experience for legitimate customers.

## tab-bank-why
title: Continuous fraud intelligence for the Nordic digital payment landscape.

RS IntelliEdge™ helps banks assess risk as transactions happen, combining behavioural analytics, AI/ML, rules and ecosystem intelligence. It supports fraud detection across A2A, wallets and other digital payment channels, helping identify unusual transaction behaviour, APP fraud and account takeover patterns while providing explainable risk decisions and a more continuous view of customer activity.

## tab-central
label: For Central
title: *Built for* an A2A-first payment ecosystem.

Across the Nordics, account-to-account payments are deeply embedded in everyday digital payments, supported by services such as Swish, Vipps and MobilePay. This creates a payment environment where fraud can move rapidly across schemes and channels.

RS IntelliEdge™ creates a unified fraud intelligence layer across A2A payments, wallets, cards and other channels, helping central infrastructures identify patterns that individual participants may not see.

## tab-central-why
title: Shared fraud intelligence for an A2A-first payment landscape.

RS IntelliEdge™ brings real-time fraud, risk and AML intelligence across the connected Nordic payment environment, combining AI/ML, behavioural analytics, rules and federated learning. It helps central infrastructures connect intelligence across A2A payments, wallets, cards and other channels, supporting faster identification of emerging fraud patterns and more explainable risk decisions.
