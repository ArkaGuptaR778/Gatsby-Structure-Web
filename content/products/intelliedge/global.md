## intro
title: RS IntelliEdge™ is RS Software's *AI/ML-driven* fraud and risk platform

RS IntelliEdge™ is RS Software's AI/ML-driven fraud and risk management solution designed for real-time protection of digital payments. It adapts to evolving threats, ensuring secure, intelligent monitoring across all transaction types. Whether you're managing national payment systems or banking operations, RS IntelliEdge™ has you covered.

## tab-bank
label: For Bank
title: *Built for* banks to protect customers in real time.

Fraud no longer stays within a single channel. RS IntelliEdge™ gives banks a unified view of risk across transactions, customers, devices, and payment channels, combining AI/ML, behavioural analytics, and rules to detect threats before funds leave the account.

From APP fraud and account takeovers to mule activity and emerging fraud patterns, banks can respond at payment speed while keeping legitimate customer journeys moving.

## tab-bank-why
title: Real-time fraud intelligence across every payment journey.

RS IntelliEdge™ helps banks assess transaction risk as payments happen, combining AI/ML, behavioural analytics, rules and ecosystem intelligence. It supports fraud detection across real-time payments, cards, A2A, wallets, and other digital payment channels, helping identify emerging fraud patterns while enabling explainable decisions and a more continuous view of customer and transaction risk.

## tab-bank-capabilities
- **Real-Time Fraud Detection** — AI-driven scoring delivers instant risk assessment for early fraud intervention and faster decisioning.
- **Cross-Channel Protection** — Monitor fraud across mobile, internet, ATM, branch and other payment channels from a unified intelligence layer.
- **Behavioural Analytics & Customer 360** — Combine transaction, behavioural, device and identity signals to detect unusual customer activity.
- **APP, ATO & Mule Detection** — Identify authorised push payment fraud, account takeovers and mule activity using behavioural and AI-powered pattern recognition.
- **Explainable AI (XAI)** — Give fraud teams human-readable explanations behind risk decisions and audit-ready trails.
- **AI/ML Model Operations** — Train, test, monitor and adapt fraud models as threat patterns change.
- **Case Management & AML Assist** — Support investigation, transaction search, case handling, entity-link analysis, and AML workflows in one environment.
- **API-First Integration** — Integrate with existing banking and payment infrastructure without requiring a complete technology overhaul.

## tab-central
label: For Central
title: *Built for* payment ecosystems that cannot afford blind spots.

Real-time payment networks operate across multiple rails, participants, and transaction types. RS IntelliEdge™ creates a unified fraud intelligence layer that helps central infrastructures detect risk across the ecosystem, not just within individual participants.

It combines real-time scoring, behavioural intelligence, AI/ML, rules, and federated learning to identify emerging threats while giving participating institutions the visibility and controls needed to act quickly.

## tab-central-why
title: A unified intelligence layer for the global real-time payments ecosystem.

RS IntelliEdge™ brings real-time fraud, risk and AML intelligence across payment networks and rails, combining AI/ML, behavioural analytics, rules and federated learning. It helps central infrastructures and payment ecosystems identify emerging fraud patterns, strengthen cross-rail intelligence and support fast, explainable and actionable risk decisions at payment speed.
