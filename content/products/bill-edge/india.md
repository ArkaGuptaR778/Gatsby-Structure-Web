## intro
title: From BBPS-scale infrastructure to *the next generation of bill payments.*

India has already demonstrated what happens when bill payments move from fragmented channels to a unified ecosystem. RS Bill@Edge™ powers the Bharat Bill Payment System (BBPS), established by NPCI, connecting consumers, banks, fintechs and billers through a nationwide bill presentment and payment environment.

Building on this experience, RS Bill@Edge™ brings biller onboarding, invoice generation, presentment, payment, reconciliation, complaints, disputes, and refunds into one connected lifecycle.

## metrics
- **~1.1 Billion+** — Bills Processed in FY2023
- **7+ Years** — Of Bill Payments in India
- **12 Million** — Transactions/Month

## why
eyebrow: Why RS Bill@Edge™
title: *Built around the scale* and operational complexity of Indian bill payments

From BBPS and NPCI-led bill payment infrastructure to the day-to-day needs of banks, billers and agents, RS Bill@Edge™ combines the operational controls, automation, and visibility needed to manage a large bill payment ecosystem.

Accelerate biller onboarding with AI-assisted payload mapping that identifies field matches and gaps for operator review, turning a configuration process that once took weeks into 1–2 working days. Configurable dashboards bring fetch, validation, payment, status, settlement, and dispute metrics into one view, while AI-assisted layouts, agent and geography heat maps, and COU/BOU dashboards help teams monitor performance, identify inactive agents, and improve operational and cost control.

## capabilities
- **Biller Onboarding** — Simplify biller onboarding, configuration and participant management through a structured, configuration-driven process.
- **Invoice Generation & Conversion** — Generate and convert invoices into structured digital payment journeys.
- **Bill Presentment** — Support consolidated bill views and digital bill presentment across channels.
- **Request for Payment** — Convert invoices into Request for Payment messages for structured collections.
- **Reconciliation** — Connect consumer-bank payment gateways with biller-side processing for reconciliation.
- **Complaints & Disputes** — Manage complaints, disputes and refund workflows within the bill payment lifecycle.
- **MIS & Notifications** — Support transaction search, notifications, custom MIS reporting and downtime notifications.
- **Payment & Settlement Visibility** — Track payment status and settlement activity across the bill payment lifecycle.

## closing
From AI-assisted onboarding to operational intelligence, RS Bill@Edge™ helps make India's bill payment ecosystem easier to configure, monitor and manage.
