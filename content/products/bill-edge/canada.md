## intro
title: Built for a payment environment moving toward *real-time, data-rich payments.*

As Canada moves toward the Payments Canada Real-Time Rail (RTR) and ISO 20022-based payment infrastructure, bill payment can become more than a payment instruction; it can become a connected, data-rich customer journey.

RS Bill@Edge™ provides an API-first bill presentment and Request for Payment layer that can sit above payment infrastructure, connecting billers, biller banks, payer banks and customers through a governed invoice-to-payment lifecycle.

## metrics
- **21,000+** — Billers
- **26+** — Biller Categories
- **Billions** — Bill Payment Interactions Annually

## why
eyebrow: Why RS Bill@Edge™
title: *Bring bill presentment and RfP* into Canada's evolving payment ecosystem

With ISO 20022 at the centre of Canada's modern payment infrastructure, RS Bill@Edge™ provides structured bill presentment and RfP orchestration while maintaining flexibility across infrastructure models.

## capabilities
- **ISO 20022 RfP** — Create structured Request for Payment journeys aligned with data-rich payment environments.
- **RTR-Ready Bill Payment Layer** — Position bill presentment and RfP orchestration above evolving real-time payment infrastructure.
- **Digital Bill Presentment** — Bring invoices into the customer's banking experience instead of fragmented biller channels.
- **Flexible Payment Journeys** — Support immediate, later and scheduled payment choices.
- **Mandate Management** — Manage recurring payments with configurable mandate lifecycle controls.
- **Payment Confirmation** — Provide closed-loop acknowledgement and status visibility across participants.
- **Reconciliation** — Automate matching and connect biller and bank-side payment information.
- **API-First Integration** — Expose platform capabilities through APIs, ISO 20022-compliant endpoints and a dedicated sandbox.
