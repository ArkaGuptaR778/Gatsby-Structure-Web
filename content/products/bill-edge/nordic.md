## intro
title: As Nordic payment ecosystems become increasingly instant and interoperable, *the bill should be just as easy to act on.*

Across the Nordic region, payment ecosystems are aligning around NPC Instant Credit Transfer and SEPA Instant standards and increasingly digital customer experiences. RS Bill@Edge™ brings that same focus to bill payments, combining digital bill presentment, Request for Payment, flexible payment journeys and intelligent bill experiences.

## metrics
- **21,000+** — Billers
- **26+** — Biller Categories
- **Billions** — Bill Payment Interactions Annually

## why
eyebrow: Why RS Bill@Edge™
title: *Turn the Nordic digital payment experience* into a smarter bill payment journey

For customers accustomed to digital, account-based payment experiences, RS Bill@Edge™ turns complex invoices into simple actions with a smart bill inbox, GenAI summaries, flexible payment choices and closed-loop confirmation.

## capabilities
- **Smart Bill Inbox** — Bring bills from multiple billers into one consolidated digital view.
- **GenAI Bill Summaries** — Surface what is due, what has changed and what needs action.
- **NPC/SEPA Instant-Ready Architecture** — Provide a rail-agnostic bill payment layer that can operate above existing payment infrastructure.
- **Request for Payment** — Turn invoices into structured digital payment requests.
- **Flexible Payment Options** — Support pay-now, pay-later and scheduled payment journeys.
- **Mandate Management** — Enable recurring payments with the ability to set up, pause, resume or modify mandates.
- **Notifications & Confirmation** — Keep customers informed and close the loop with biller-confirmed payment acknowledgement.
- **Intelligent Servicing** — Manage corrections, refunds, reversals, disputes and exceptions within one governed lifecycle.
