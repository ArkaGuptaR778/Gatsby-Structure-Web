## intro
title: Every bill. Every participant. *Every market.*

Payment rails have evolved, but the bill payment journey often remains fragmented across invoices, biller portals, payment channels and reconciliation systems. RS Bill@Edge™ brings these pieces together through a unified, API-first platform for bill presentment and Request for Payment (RfP)-led bill payments.

From invoice generation and presentment to payment orchestration, servicing, reconciliation and intelligence, RS Bill@Edge™ manages the complete invoice-to-action lifecycle across payment infrastructures and market models.

## metrics
- **21,000+** — Billers
- **26+** — Biller Categories
- **Billions** — Bill Payment Interactions Annually

## why
eyebrow: Why RS Bill@Edge™

RS Bill@Edge™ is rail-agnostic and deployment-model agnostic, allowing it to sit above existing payment infrastructure as an orchestration and experience layer or operate as a switch where required. Its four-party model connects billers, biller banks, payer banks and customers through one governed, traceable lifecycle.

## capabilities
- **Invoice-to-Action** — Ingest, validate and normalize invoices from ERPs, bulk files, biller portals and APIs.
- **Smart Bill Presentment** — Give customers a consolidated bill inbox with GenAI-powered summaries.
- **ISO 20022 RfP** — Generate and orchestrate structured Request for Payment messages.
- **Flexible Payments** — Enable customers to pay immediately, later or on a scheduled basis.
- **Mandate Management** — Support recurring payments with configurable mandate lifecycle controls.
- **Reconciliation & Confirmation** — Connect payment processing, reconciliation and real-time status visibility.
- **Payment Intelligence** — Use payment certainty scoring, exception prediction and next-best-action insights.
