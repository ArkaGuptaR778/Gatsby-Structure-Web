## intro
title: Bring RFP-led bill payments and everyday payment choices *into one connected journey.*

The U.S. payment ecosystem spans FedNow® RFP, ACH, cards, wallets, bank transfers and other local payment channels. RS Bill@Edge™ provides a rail-agnostic bill payment layer that connects invoice creation and presentment with Request for Payment, flexible payment options, reconciliation and payment visibility.

For SMBs and gig workers, the platform also brings mobile-first invoice creation and AI-assisted drafting into the payment journey.

## metrics
- **21,000+** — Billers
- **26+** — Biller Categories
- **Billions** — Bill Payment Interactions Annually

## why
eyebrow: Why RS Bill@Edge™
title: *Give every U.S. invoice* a clearer path to payment

Whether a customer pays through a banking app using Request for Payment, or chooses cards, wallets, bank transfers or other local payment methods, RS Bill@Edge™ connects the invoice to the payment experience while giving the biller visibility into what happens next.

## capabilities
- **RFP-Led Bill Payments** — Turn invoices into digital Request for Payment journeys, including use cases aligned with RFP-enabled real-time payment environments.
- **Multi-Channel Payment Choice** — Support banking apps, cards, wallets, bank transfers and local payment rails.
- **Mobile-First Invoicing** — Enable SMBs and gig workers to create professional invoices directly from their phones.
- **AI-Assisted Invoice Creation** — Generate invoices from plain-language input and reduce the effort involved in billing.
- **Digital Bill Presentment** — Deliver invoices through digital channels and create a connected view-and-act experience.
- **Real-Time Payment Visibility** — Track paid, pending and overdue invoices through a connected status view.
- **Reconciliation & Confirmation** — Connect payment processing with reconciliation and biller-confirmed acknowledgement.
- **Payment Intelligence** — Use payment certainty scoring and next-best-action insights to support more predictable collections.
