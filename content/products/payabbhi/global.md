## intro
title: Payabbhi® — Own Your Payments. *Build Your Merchant Payments Business.*

Payabbhi® is a cloud-native, API-first merchant payments platform that brings payment acceptance, merchant operations, partner management, transaction processing, and revenue control together on one extensible platform.

Built for PSPs, PayFacs, banks, ISOs, ISVs, and merchant-serving ecosystems, it helps organizations move beyond fragmented payment tooling to build, operate, and scale branded payment propositions with greater control over merchant relationships, payment experiences, and economics.

## why
eyebrow: Why Payabbhi®
title: *One platform* for the full merchant payment lifecycle

Payabbhi® brings onboarding, acceptance, processing, settlement, reconciliation, partner management, and revenue control into one platform. API-first capabilities help accelerate merchant onboarding, while built-in analytics and operational tools improve visibility.

Flexible pricing, partner and revenue-share models give you greater control over payment economics, while white-label capabilities help create differentiated payment experiences under your brand.

## capabilities

- **Omnichannel Payment Acceptance** — Accept payments across POS, e-commerce, mobile commerce, apps, hosted payment pages, payment links, and QR-based journeys.
- **Merchant Management** — Manage merchant onboarding, underwriting, configuration, activation, and ongoing merchant operations from a unified platform.
- **Partner & Hierarchy Management** — Support ISOs, ISVs, PayFacs, and multi-entity ecosystems with structured partner relationships, permissions, and downstream control.
- **Transaction Management** — Orchestrate payment processing across acceptance channels with transaction visibility, business rules, authentication, tokenization and operational controls.
- **Settlement & Reconciliation** — Bring settlement, clearing, reconciliation and transaction visibility together to reduce fragmented operations and improve financial control.
- **Fraud, Security & Tokenization** — Support secure payment experiences with tokenized data handling, fraud management, configurable controls, and auditability.
- **Developer & API Enablement** — REST APIs, developer portal, sandbox capabilities, documentation, and modular services help teams integrate and extend payment experiences.
- **Revenue & Commercial Management** — Manage merchant pricing, fees, funding, partner structures, and revenue-share models with greater visibility into payment economics.
