## intro
title: Payabbhi® — *Modernising Merchant Acquiring* for India

India’s payment ecosystem is evolving rapidly, with high-volume digital acceptance and increasingly sophisticated models across banks, PSPs, PayFacs, and ISVs.

Payabbhi® brings merchant onboarding, KYC, underwriting, fraud management, payment acceptance, transaction processing, settlement, reconciliation, and analytics onto a unified platform, helping organizations simplify payment operations and gain greater control over merchants, data, and the payment lifecycle.

## metrics
- **60 min** — Merchant Onboarding — 70% demonstrated reduction in merchant onboarding time
- **~95.7%** — Success Rate — With transaction processing in under 20 seconds
- **99.995%** — Uptime — 3M transactions processed per month

## why
eyebrow: Why Payabbhi®
title: *Built for* India’s evolving merchant payments ecosystem.

Payabbhi® combines API-first architecture with the capabilities needed to onboard, operate, and manage merchants at scale. For PSPs, acquiring banks and payment-led businesses, API-based onboarding and KYC automation help simplify merchant activation, while integrated support for underwriting, processor connectivity, equipment management and payment operations keeps the merchant lifecycle connected.

Payment, settlement, payout and transaction data come together to provide clearer reconciliation and operational visibility, while configurable fraud controls, including velocity checks, rules, alerts and analytics, help organizations manage payment risk with greater precision.

## capabilities

- **Merchant Onboarding & KYC** — Digitise merchant onboarding with API-first workflows, documentation, role-based controls and configurable account setup.
- **Merchant Underwriting** — Support risk-based merchant onboarding and account configuration as part of the acquiring operating model.
- **Fraud & Risk Controls** — Apply velocity checks, configurable rules, alerts and analytics to strengthen payment-risk management.
- **Transaction & Payment Operations** — Track payment events across authorization, capture, refund, and other transaction stages with unified operational visibility.
- **Settlement & Reconciliation** — Support clearing, settlement, payout, and reconciliation workflows while maintaining visibility across payment and merchant data.
- **Merchant Analytics & Reporting** — Provide dashboards, transaction analytics, audit trails, and reporting to help payment teams understand merchant performance.
- **Tokenization & Recurring Experiences** — Use tokenized payment data and wallet capabilities to support stored-payment and recurring-payment experiences.
