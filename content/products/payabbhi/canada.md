## intro
title: Payabbhi® — Modern Merchant Payments for *Canada’s Evolving Payment Landscape*

Canada’s payment infrastructure is evolving toward richer, more structured messaging, with ISO 20022 underpinning modern systems such as the Real-Time Rail (RTR) and enabling greater interoperability and transparency. Payabbhi® complements this evolution with an API-first merchant payments platform that connects acceptance, onboarding, partner management, fraud controls, tokenization, settlement, reconciliation, and analytics.

For PSPs, PayFacs, ISVs and merchant-serving organizations, this creates a more connected alternative to fragmented payment operations, helping them build differentiated payment experiences while maintaining visibility and control across merchants, partners and transactions.

## why
eyebrow: Why Payabbhi®
title: *Turn richer payment data* into better merchant operations.

As Canada’s payment environment becomes more connected and data-rich, Payabbhi® provides an API-first, ISO 20022-ready approach that helps payment providers make better use of payment and remittance data for processing and reconciliation.

By bringing acceptance, merchant and partner management, settlement and analytics together, it reduces reliance on fragmented payment stacks, while hierarchical permissions and unified settlement and reconciliation workflows provide greater visibility and control across PSPs, ISOs, ISVs, merchants and payment partners.

## capabilities

- **Omnichannel Acceptance** — Support POS, e-commerce, mobile commerce, in-app journeys, hosted pages and payment links through one acceptance platform.
- **API-First Payment Infrastructure** — Use modular APIs and integration-ready services to build payment experiences around evolving Canadian payment requirements.
- **Tokenization & Secure Payments** — Support secure payment-data handling with tokenization and enterprise-grade security controls.
- **Merchant Onboarding & Operations** — Manage merchant activation, underwriting, configuration and ongoing merchant lifecycle operations.
- **Partner & Permission Management** — Give payment providers visibility and control across partners, merchants and multi-entity operating structures.
- **Fraud & Risk Management** — Support configurable fraud rules, alerts, velocity checks and payment-risk controls within the merchant operating layer.
- **Settlement & Reconciliation** — Automate and monitor settlement, reconciliation and funding workflows across merchant and partner relationships.
- **Analytics & Commercial Control** — Combine reporting, transaction analytics, pricing, fees and revenue-share capabilities to improve visibility into merchant economics.
