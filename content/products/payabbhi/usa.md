## intro
title: Payabbhi® — Build a *Smarter Merchant Payments Business* in the U.S.

The U.S. merchant payments landscape demands flexible acceptance, secure checkout and seamless integration across PSPs, PayFacs, acquirers and ISVs. Payabbhi® provides a modular, API-first platform for cards, ACH, e-commerce, POS, embedded and recurring payments, bringing merchant operations, fraud management, reconciliation and settlement together in one operating layer.

Its U.S. relevance is demonstrated through government-focused utility and eCheck payment implementations, including split funding for bill amounts and convenience fees.

## why
eyebrow: Why Payabbhi®
title: *Compete with* speed, control and payment intelligence.

For U.S. PSPs, ISOs and PayFacs, differentiation goes beyond the transaction itself. Payabbhi® combines API-first integration with REST APIs, Swagger and sandbox capabilities, helping ISVs, PSPs and PayFacs connect faster without legacy complexity.

Once connected, tokenization, fraud controls, velocity checks, configurable rules and alerts strengthen payment security, while card and ACH support brings settlement, reconciliation and reporting into the same flow. Flexible merchant pricing, fees, partner structures, funding and split-funding models then give providers greater control over their commercial operations.

## capabilities

- **Card & ACH Acceptance** — Support card and ACH payment journeys across physical and digital merchant environments.
- **Hosted Checkout & Payment Links** — Provide branded payment collection through hosted pages, payment links and other low-friction acceptance experiences.
- **Embedded Payments** — Embed payment capabilities directly into software, applications and merchant journeys.
- **Tokenization & Secure Checkout** — Protect sensitive payment data through tokenized payment handling and secure payment experiences.
- **Fraud Management** — Apply velocity checks, configurable rules, alerts and fraud controls to identify and manage suspicious payment activity.
- **Merchant & Partner Management** — Manage merchants, ISOs, partners and downstream entities with role-based access and hierarchical controls.
- **Settlement & Reconciliation** — Support automated settlement, multi-layer reconciliation, transaction closing, and financial visibility across payment operations.
- **Revenue & Split-Funding Control** — Support flexible commercial models, including fee management and split funding for use cases where transaction components must be routed to different downstream accounts.
