Sunetra Bhattacharya brings nearly two decades of HR leadership experience in the Software and Services industry and now supports RS Software as an HR Leadership Consultant. In this role, she provides strategic guidance on people development, performance management, leadership growth, and culture-building initiatives, ensuring that the company’s talent strategy stays closely aligned with its business goals.

Her consulting contributions span coaching, mentoring, performance frameworks, and Diversity, Equity & Inclusion (DEI) advocacy, helping RS Software nurture a high-performance, values-driven culture. With deep expertise in talent management, Sunetra advises on designing and implementing frameworks that foster growth, recognition, and retention, strengthening RS Software’s ability to cultivate a resilient, future-ready workforce.

A psychologist by training, Sunetra holds a master’s degree in applied psychology from Calcutta University and a PGDBA in HR from Symbiosis Centre for Distance Learning. She is a Certified POSH & POCSO Trainer, AON Certified Distinguished Behavioral Event Interviewing Expert, certified Lead Auditor (ISO 9001:2015), NLP Practitioner, and recipient of the HRAI Woman Achiever 2023 award. Her diverse credentials reflect her deep commitment to building impactful, human-centered organizations.
