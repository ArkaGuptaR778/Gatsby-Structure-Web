With over 30 years of leadership experience spanning global technology giants such as Microsoft and Oracle, Samik Roy brings to RS Software a rare blend of strategic vision, operational excellence, and transformative execution. His career is distinguished by successfully leading Billion-dollar businesses, driving bold innovation, and delivering sustained hypergrowth across diverse products, industries, and market segments. Widely recognized for his expertise in AI, digital transformation, revenue management, and global partnerships, Samik has a proven track record of orchestrating business turnarounds and positioning organizations for long-term success. His leadership has consistently earned global recognition for each of the businesses that he has led at Microsoft.

At RS Software, Samik serves as Chief Operating Officer, where he spearheads global revenue, strategy, product innovation, international partnerships, and project delivery. His focus is on reinforcing RS Software’s position as a trusted partner to national payment systems, core digital infrastructure, and financial institutions in India and worldwide.

Beyond RS Software, Samik serves as a Non-Executive Director on the Board of EmployWise™, an AI-driven HR SaaS product company. His leadership extends beyond the corporate realm, and he is on the Advisory Council of Breakthrough™, a non-profit organization dedicated to eradicating violence and discrimination against women and girls.
