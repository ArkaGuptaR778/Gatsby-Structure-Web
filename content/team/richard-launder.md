Richard Launder has more than 20 years of experience in the payments industry with strengths in sales, marketing and operations. As a managing director in Europe, Middle East and Africa for one of the largest software companies in the payments industry, Mr. Launder generated more than $130 million in revenue by developing a distribution model for the region based on acquisitions, direct operations, joint ventures, distributors and sales agents.

He is expert in strategic selling, building effective sales teams and marketing within diverse markets and cultures. Mr. Launder was recently added to the RS Software board to help the company continue to develop growth strategies for the business.
